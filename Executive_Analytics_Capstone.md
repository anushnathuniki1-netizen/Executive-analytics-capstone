# Executive Analytics Capstone

## Customer Churn Analysis

**Business Analytics Project**

Objective: Understand customer churn patterns and recommend actions to improve customer retention.

---

# 1. Business Problem

- Customer churn affects business growth.
- Losing customers can reduce monthly revenue.
- Businesses need to understand why customers leave.
- Data analysis helps identify customer behavior patterns.

**Business Question:** How can the business improve customer retention?

---

# 2. Dataset Overview

- Dataset: Customer Churn Sample
- Total records: 15
- Total columns: 11
- Customer information and subscription details
- Support tickets and payment information
- Churn status: Yes / No

**Tools Used:**
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Streamlit

---

# 3. Overall Churn Analysis

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Customers | 15 |
| Churned Customers | 7 |
| Retained Customers | 8 |
| Churn Rate | 46.67% |

**Finding:**

Almost 47% of the customers in this sample have churned.

---

# 4. Five Important Business Insights

1. Churn rate is 46.67%.
2. Churned customers stayed for an average of 7 months.
3. Retained customers stayed for an average of 29.12 months.
4. Churned customers raised an average of 4.29 support tickets.
5. Retained customers raised an average of 1 support ticket.

**Key Finding:** Customer tenure and support tickets show notable differences between retained and churned customers.

---

# 5. Revenue Analysis

| Metric | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Average monthly charges of churned customers | $58.56 |
| Estimated 12-month charges | $702.72 |

**Illustrative Impact:**

Retaining one similar customer for another 12 months could preserve approximately $702.72 in billed charges, assuming the monthly charge remains unchanged.

This is an illustrative estimate, not guaranteed revenue.

---

# 6. Business Recommendations

- Identify customers with shorter subscription tenure.
- Improve customer support services.
- Resolve repeated customer complaints.
- Provide suitable retention offers.
- Monitor customer churn regularly.

**Goal:** Improve customer satisfaction and retention.

---

# 7. Limitations

- The dataset contains only 15 records.
- The sample may not represent the entire customer base.
- Results show relationships, not proven causes.
- Future churn cannot be predicted reliably from this small sample.
- Business impact estimates depend on assumptions.

More customer data is needed for reliable conclusions.

---

# 8. Conclusion and Expected Impact

**Expected Business Impact:**

- Better customer retention.
- Improved customer support.
- Reduced potential revenue loss.
- Better understanding of customer behavior.
- Improved business decisions using data.

**Final Conclusion:**

Customer churn analysis helps businesses identify important customer behavior patterns and take suitable steps to improve retention.
